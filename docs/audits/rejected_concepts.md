# Rejected Concepts

Ideas explicitly considered and abandoned. Recorded so they are not accidentally
resurrected. Each is <span class="sq-badge rejected">REJECTED</span>.

- **The present conflict and the Devastation War are the same war.** Superseded by the
  two-war structure.
- **Continuity causes Devastation Day.** Reversed: Continuity is *born from* Devastation.
- **Continuity as evil AI.** It is a defensible, sometimes-correct survival calculator.
- **Markus as an 'AI who wants to be human.'** He does not aspire to be human.
- **A single mystical instant where Markus 'becomes alive.'** No such moment exists.
- **M.A.R.K.U.S. as secretly sentient.** It is a substrate.
- **The older man as Greg's 'best friend.'** Greg admires him and overestimates the closeness.
- **The House-on-the-Hill family as a secret conspiracy** / secretly responsible for the war.
- **Childhood Greg as adult Greg in miniature** (cynical, profane, rebellious).
- **Young Markus as mischievous/delinquent.**
- **The White Ship as conclusively:** his ghost · digital data · another AI · divine · alien technology.
- **The White Ship as an automatic guardian angel** — it does not save Markus.
- **Greg simply following a dead mentor home in the finale.**
