# Older Man's Departure

The [older man](../characters/older_man.md)'s path from the [House on the Hill](house_on_the_hill.md)
to command, and out of the story's visible world.

## Established <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

He graduates an elite academy, obtains a prestigious command, rises through the
[Devastation War](../world/devastation_war.md), and **disappears during [Devastation Day](devastation_day.md)**.
He later appears only ambiguously in official memorial records.

## Open <span class="sq-badge open">OPEN</span>

The texture of his rise and departure, and how much Greg witnesses versus learns later.
His ambiguous later association with the [White Ship](../mystery/white_ship.md) is
<span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span> and must never resolve into 'he is the ship.'
