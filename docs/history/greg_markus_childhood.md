# Greg + Markus Childhood

<span class="sq-status">Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> (characterization), with ages as a working range</span>

The childhood material establishes the bond that is the book's spine, and the *kind*
of people Greg and Markus start as — before the war, before the ranks, before the loss.

## Setting

A far-future human [colony world](../world/colony_world.md) — **not Earth** — that has
been home long enough that children think nothing of it. Greg's region feels rural and
agricultural (farmland, streams, small roads, local businesses, a tight-knit community,
children on bicycles), yet it is unmistakably far-future (orbital traffic, advanced
automation, synthetic workers, interplanetary logistics). *Reference feel: a Smallville-
like rural childhood, on another world, centuries from now.*

## Young Greg <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

Roughly 10–12 in the early material. Curious, earnest, gentle, socially inexperienced,
awkward; confident partly because he doesn't yet recognize where he doesn't belong;
fascinated by technology, people, and places beyond his ordinary life.

He is **not** adult Greg in miniature — not cynical, not profane, not deliberately
rebellious. His defiance develops later.

## Young Markus <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

**Not** mischievous. He does not encourage stealing, crime, malicious rule-breaking,
delinquency, or reckless behavior for entertainment. He is curious and capable of
disagreement, but he trusts Greg enormously and generally follows because Greg is his
social anchor.

That excessive trust eventually contributes to serious trouble during adolescence,
when Greg begins challenging rules and laws. *(Developed later.)* Young Markus does
**not** yet speak like adult First Commander Markus; his behavioral sophistication
develops over time.
