# Markus Origin

<span class="sq-status">Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> (core), with one provisional detail (the M.A.R.K.U.S. expansion)</span>

The full account of how a written-off machine became Markus. This is the seed of the
**recovery motif** and the anchor of Markus's personhood.

## The facility and the write-off <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

[Adam](../characters/adam.md), Greg's father, works at a major industrial/logistics
facility supporting the colony. The facility uses small utility robots for work in
spaces inconvenient for adult humans or larger machinery — deliberately short and
compact machines.

One unit is damaged and economically written off. Its proprietary company/manufacturer
software is wiped before it can leave the facility. Adam casually asks a coworker what
will happen to it. The coworker effectively says:

> "You want it? Take it. Just wipe it before it leaves."

## Bringing it home <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

Adam brings the damaged unit home because he finds the compact machine amusing and
thinks Greg will enjoy it. It is light enough to carry by a built-in handle on its
rear frame.

Greg reacts almost like a child whose father brought home a puppy — immediate
fascination. He wants it working. But the machine has hardware and **no meaningful
operating personality** after the proprietary wipe.

## The substrate: M.A.R.K.U.S. <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

Adam searches his workshop and finds an old software package he had saved:
**M.A.R.K.U.S.**

Provisional expansion <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>:
*Modular Adaptive Runtime Kernel for Utility Systems.*

M.A.R.K.U.S. is old, modular, unusually powerful, flexible, resource-heavy,
inconvenient, originally used for mundane utility/appliance purposes, and never widely
adopted because lighter systems were more practical.

!!! warning "It is not secretly sentient"
    M.A.R.K.U.S. is a **substrate**, not a hidden soul. Adam adapts it to the damaged
    robot; the system activates; the framework identifies itself as M.A.R.K.U.S. Greg
    hears **Markus.** The technical designation becomes a name.

## Growth over years <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

- Adam repairs hardware.
- Greg interacts with Markus constantly, adds and modifies systems.
- Markus accumulates persistent experiences.
- Others contribute hardware — the [young man at the House on the Hill](../characters/older_man.md)
  later improves Markus's legs, balance geometry, and servos.
- Markus physically grows alongside Greg through progressively taller, more capable bodies.
- Eventually, Markus begins modifying the M.A.R.K.U.S. architecture itself.

That last step is the threshold of **[self-authorship](self_authorship.md).**

## Conceptual lineage

> Adam recovered the machine. M.A.R.K.U.S. gave it a framework. Greg gave Markus a
> life. Other people helped him move through the world. Eventually Markus began
> authoring himself.

!!! danger "Do not mystify"
    There is **no single confirmed instant** where Markus "became alive." This is not
    a mystical consciousness event and not an AI-wants-to-be-human story.
