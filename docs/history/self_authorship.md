# Self-Authorship

The threshold at which [Markus](../characters/markus.md) began modifying the
**M.A.R.K.U.S.** architecture *itself* — and so began authoring who he is.

## Established <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

Over years of accumulated experience, hardware growth, and constant interaction with
[Greg](../characters/greg.md), Markus crossed from *running* the M.A.R.K.U.S. substrate
to *rewriting* it. He owns his cognition, memory, and identity.

!!! danger "No single instant"
    There is **no confirmed moment** where Markus 'became alive.' Self-authorship is a
    process, not an event, and M.A.R.K.U.S. was never secretly sentient. See
    [Narrative Rules](../foundation/narrative_rules.md).
