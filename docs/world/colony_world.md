# The Colony World

<span class="sq-status">Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> (setting), name <span class="sq-badge open">OPEN</span></span>

## The core fact

This story does **not** take place on Earth. Greg grows up on a far-future human
**colony world** — broadly Earth-like and habitable, settled long enough that children
consider it home.

## Texture: rural surface, far-future bones <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

The region where Greg grows up has a rural, agricultural atmosphere:

- farmland, streams, small roads
- local businesses and a tight-knit community
- children traveling by bicycle
- agricultural infrastructure

But the setting is unmistakably far future:

- orbital traffic
- advanced industrial automation
- synthetic workers
- interplanetary logistics
- colonial infrastructure
- high technology integrated into ordinary life

> Visual / emotional reference: a Smallville-like rural childhood, on another world,
> hundreds of years in the future.

The contrast is the point — the ordinary and the advanced share the same street. A boy
on a peeling red bicycle passes under orbital traffic and thinks nothing of it.
