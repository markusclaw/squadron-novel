# The Frontier War

<span class="sq-status">Status: <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span> (→ CANON once the [timeline](../history/master_timeline.md) is locked)</span>

The **primary military conflict of Book One.** It occurs several years after the
[Devastation War](devastation_war.md) and partially emerges from unresolved
Devastation-era political and strategic decisions.

## What is established <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

- It is the present-day war the squadron fights.
- It is causally downstream of Devastation — the earlier war's unstable armistice and
  unresolved choices feed it.
- It is the arena in which Greg tests his belief in **operational autonomy** through
  **[URRD](urrd_command.md)**.

## What is open <span class="sq-badge open">OPEN</span>

- The precise inciting incident and opening moves.
- The belligerents, the front, and the strategic geography.
- How the war's pressure maps onto the [Active Counter](../mystery/white_ship.md) beats
  (Markus's loss, return, and the finale).

!!! warning
    Do not resolve Devastation Day or invent precise dates in order to "explain" the
    Frontier War's origin. The causal link is established; the specifics are
    intentionally still open.
