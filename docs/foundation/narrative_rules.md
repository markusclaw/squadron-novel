# Narrative Rules

<span class="sq-status">Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> — hard constraints that protect the story's integrity. Breaking these is a continuity error.</span>

These are the "do not" lines. They exist because each one, if crossed, quietly
wrecks something the story depends on.

## On Markus and personhood

- Markus is **not** an "AI who wants to be human." Do not write that story.
- There is **no single instant** where Markus "became alive." No mystical
  consciousness event. He is a substrate that accumulated experience and eventually
  authored himself.
- M.A.R.K.U.S. is **not secretly sentient**. It is a flexible, resource-heavy legacy
  runtime — a substrate, not a soul.

## On Continuity

- Continuity is **not** evil AI. It can be correct; it genuinely saves lives.
- Continuity did **not** cause Devastation Day. Continuity was *born from* Devastation.
- It is **not** equivalent to Markus.

## On the antagonist

- Veyr is **not** a mastermind secretly controlling everything. He defends Continuity
  from conviction and grief, not villainy.

## On the White Ship

- **Do not solve it.** Do not identify its true nature.
- It is not conclusively his ghost, digital data, another AI, divine, or alien tech.
- It does **not** reliably rescue everyone — and it does **not** save Markus.
- The finale is SQUADRON opening the path; the White Ship joins them. Greg does not
  simply follow a dead mentor home.

## On the childhood material

- Childhood Greg is a curious, gentle, socially inexperienced tween — **not** adult
  Greg in a smaller body, not cynical, not profane, not deliberately rebellious.
- Young Markus is **not** mischievous or delinquent. He trusts Greg and follows him.
  Neither child yet speaks or behaves like their adult self.

## On the House on the Hill

- The older man is **not** described as Greg's "best friend."
- The family is **not** a secret conspiracy family, and is **not** secretly
  responsible for the war.

## On the recovery motif

- *Something declared not worth recovering is recovered anyway* is **structural DNA,
  not dialogue.** Never state it to the reader.

## On writing pace (current phase)

- <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> for FOUNDATION v0.2: **do not write
  Chapter One prose yet.** Do not invent the planet name, resolve Devastation Day,
  determine the White Ship's nature, invent precise calendar dates, overdefine URRD,
  finalize First Commander terminology, or create filler minor characters.
