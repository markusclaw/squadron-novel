# Adam

<span class="sq-status">Role: Greg's father · Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> (core)</span>

## Snapshot

Greg's father. The first link in the recovery chain: he brought home a written-off
machine because he thought his son would enjoy it, and unknowingly began Markus.

## Established <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

- Works at a major industrial/logistics facility supporting the colony. The facility
  uses small, deliberately short and compact utility robots for work in spaces
  inconvenient for adult humans or larger machinery.
- When one unit was damaged and economically written off, Adam casually asked a
  coworker what would happen to it. The coworker effectively said: *"You want it? Take
  it. Just wipe it before it leaves."*
- He brought the damaged unit home because he found the compact machine amusing and
  thought Greg would enjoy it. It was light enough to carry by a built-in handle on
  its rear frame.
- The machine had hardware but no meaningful operating personality after the wipe.
  Adam searched his workshop and found an old saved software package — **M.A.R.K.U.S.**
  — and adapted it to the robot. The system activated and identified itself.
- Over the following years, Adam repaired and maintained Markus's hardware.

## Character note

Adam acts casually, almost offhandedly — the puppy-from-work energy — not as someone
who believes he is creating a person. His ordinariness is the point: personhood here
begins in a garage, not a laboratory.
