# Greg

<span class="sq-status">Role: lead, POV · Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> (core), with provisional arc detail</span>

## Snapshot

Human. Grew up on a far-future human colony world (not Earth), alongside the
synthetic person **Markus**. The emotional spine of the book is his bond with Markus.

## Childhood characterization <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

In the early childhood material (roughly 10–12 years old), Greg is:

- highly curious
- earnest
- gentle
- socially inexperienced and awkward
- confident partly because he does not yet recognize where he doesn't belong
- fascinated by technology, people, and environments beyond his ordinary life

He is **not** yet adult Greg in miniature. Not cynical. Not profane. Not deliberately
rebellious. His later defiance develops over time — and his adolescent challenging of
rules and laws is what eventually pulls the trusting Markus into serious trouble.
*(That material is developed later.)*

## Want — Book One <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

Greg does not primarily want rank. He wants **operational autonomy**:

> Give capable people the objective and enough information. Allow those closest to
> the problem to decide how to solve it. Centralized systems frequently fail because
> they operate too far from consequences.

**URRD** gives Greg the opportunity to prove this philosophy.

## Need / arc <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

His arc must challenge whether instinct and conviction are *sufficient*. The story
pressures the gap between what he believes (autonomy always wins) and what he will
have to learn (its limits, its costs, when closeness to the problem is not enough).

- **Start state:** convinced, capable, un-tested at scale.
- **End state:** <span class="sq-badge open">OPEN</span> — determined by how the
  Frontier War and the loss of Markus test the conviction.

## Key relationships

- **[Markus](markus.md)** — grew up together; the spine. Inside SQUADRON, Markus
  *chooses* to accept Greg's tactical lead.
- **[Adam](adam.md)** — father, who brought Markus home.
- **[The Older Man](older_man.md)** — admired childhood figure from the House on the Hill.

## Command relationship <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

Markus may outrank Greg institutionally, yet in combat operations follows Greg's
tactical leadership by choice. This matters precisely because Markus is self-owned.
