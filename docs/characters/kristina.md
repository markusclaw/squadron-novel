# Kristina

<span class="sq-status">Role: Command operations coordinator · Status: <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span> (placement)</span>

## Snapshot

Command's forward-embedded coordinator with the SQUADRON detachment — close enough to
be family, connected enough to hold the larger strategic picture. Her presence makes
the Relay failure *hurt.*

## Preferred placement <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

Kristina is a Command operations coordinator **forward-embedded aboard the SQUADRON
detachment's carrier/tender.** Because of that placement she can:

- attend ceremonies
- eat with the crew and enter the galley
- work directly with them day to day
- maintain the larger strategic picture

## The Relay device <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

When the squadron loses **Relay** connectivity, **Kristina disappears from their
communications.** This is deliberate: it makes Relay failure emotionally tangible —
the loss of the network is the loss of *her voice*, not just a status indicator.
