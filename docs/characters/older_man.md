# The Older Man

<span class="sq-status">Role: admired mentor figure from the House on the Hill · Status: <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> (core), with provisional links</span>

!!! note "On naming"
    Referred to here as "the older man" / "the young man at the House on the Hill."
    He is **not** described as Greg's "best friend." <span class="sq-badge rejected">REJECTED</span>

## Snapshot

Several years older than Greg, he lived with an influential family on a large property
near the town. To young Greg he is a figure of admiration — and Greg, being a child,
likely **overestimates** the closeness of the relationship. This is intentional.

## Established <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

- Genuinely gracious; treats Greg and Markus naturally, never as objects of ridicule.
- Technically gifted and mechanically intuitive; encourages young Greg.
- **Later helped improve Markus's legs, balance geometry, and servos.**
- Graduated from an elite military academy.
- Obtained a prestigious command appointment; eventually became a significant
  squadron commander.
- **Disappeared during Devastation Day.**
- Later appears only *ambiguously* in official memorial records.

## The family <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> / <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

Wealthy, successful, politically and socially connected, accustomed to formal social
settings, connected to military/government/industrial circles.

- <span class="sq-badge rejected">REJECTED</span> They are **not** a secret conspiracy
  family, and **not** secretly responsible for the war.
- <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span> The father holds an important
  colonial-development / industrial / strategic role.

## Connection to the White Ship <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

The White Ship eventually behaves in ways *associated with* the older man — but it is
**not merely his ghost**, and its nature is never solved. See [White Ship](../mystery/white_ship.md).
