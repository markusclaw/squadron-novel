## How this archive works

Every significant story fact carries a **canon status**. Nothing here becomes true by accident.

<p>
<span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span> accepted and authoritative &nbsp;
<span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span> current preferred direction, still changeable &nbsp;
<span class="sq-badge open">OPEN</span> intentionally undecided &nbsp;
<span class="sq-badge rejected">REJECTED</span> considered and abandoned
</p>

Start with the [Foundation](foundation/index.md) to understand what the book *is*, or jump to the [Canon Ledger](CANON_LEDGER.md) for the fast index of what is currently true. The [Decisions](DECISIONS.md) log records *why* each architectural choice was made, and [Open Questions](foundation/unresolved.md) lists what we have deliberately left undecided.

!!! tip "Finding things"
