## What SQUADRON Is

A far-future military story exploring the clash between autonomous decision-making and algorithmic optimization.

Greg commands SQUADRON during the Frontier War. When Relay connectivity fails, SQUADRON becomes isolated. Greg must prove his autonomy philosophy works. The story tests this through catastrophic loss, impossible decisions, and the appearance of an unclassifiable phenomenon.

**Core Conflict:** Autonomy vs. Optimization  
**Philosophical Question:** Who decides what is worth recovering?
