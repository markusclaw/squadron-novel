# The White Ship

<span class="sq-status">Status: nature <span class="sq-badge open">OPEN</span> — **permanently, by authorial rule.** Behaviors are canon; the explanation does not exist.</span>

!!! danger "Do not solve it"
    Do **not** identify its true nature. There is no hidden answer waiting to be
    revealed — not even in author-only material. Its power comes from remaining
    unclassifiable.

## Established behavior <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

- Predates the [older man](../characters/older_man.md).
- Appears in historical pilot **folklore**.
- Appears around **unrecoverable** situations.
- **Physically affects reality** — it is not a hallucination or a story.
- **Cannot be cleanly classified.**
- Occasionally appears to help.
- **Does not reliably rescue everyone.**

## What it is *not* <span class="sq-badge rejected">REJECTED</span>

It is **not** conclusively any of these:

- merely the older man's ghost
- digital data
- another AI
- divine
- alien technology

Eventually it behaves in ways *associated with* the older man
<span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span> — but it is **not merely him.**

## The Markus loss encounter <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

There is a substantial White Ship encounter around Markus's catastrophic loss.

**Critical:** it does **not** save Markus. This is deliberate — it prevents the
phenomenon from becoming an automatic guardian angel. (Markus is instead brought home
later by [Rafa](../characters/rafa.md).)

## The finale <span class="sq-badge provisional">PROVISIONAL</span>

- The finale involves **SQUADRON opening the path.** The White Ship **joins** them.
- Greg does **not** simply follow a dead mentor home.
- System initially detects `UNKNOWN CONTACT`. Markus cannot explain it. Markus
  deliberately classifies it `FRIENDLY`. Eventually: `SQUADRON // 5 ACTIVE`.

!!! note "Thematic weight (do not state in prose)"
    The synthetic person humanity once struggled to classify becomes the one willing to
    classify the unclassifiable presence as *belonging.* Never explain this connection
    to the reader.
