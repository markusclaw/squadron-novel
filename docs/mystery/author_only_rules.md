# Author-Only Rules

## The public-safety rule <span class="sq-badge canon">CANON</span>

For now, author-only sections are limited to **structural rules** — how the story is
built — **not secret answers.** This is safe because:

- The [White Ship](white_ship.md)'s true nature **does not exist as a solved answer**.
  There is nothing to leak.
- Every "reveal" in the story is a *structural* choice (what the reader learns and
  when), not a hidden solution.

## Structural rules a writer needs (safe to keep here)

- The White Ship is never explained. Do not write toward an explanation.
- It does not save Markus at his loss; the recovery comes through [Rafa](../characters/rafa.md).
- The finale is SQUADRON opening the path and the ship *joining* — not Greg following a
  dead mentor home.
- The [recovery motif](../CANON_LEDGER.md) is structural DNA, never stated to the reader.
- The Active-Counter classification (`UNKNOWN` → `FRIENDLY` → `5 ACTIVE`) carries its
  thematic weight silently.

## If the repo/site is ever made private

Only then may genuinely sensitive planning be added — and even then, prefer keeping the
White Ship unsolved, because its unsolvability is the design, not a secret being kept.
