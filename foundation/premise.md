# Premise

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

---

## Core Concept

**SQUADRON** is a science-fiction novel about a far-future military unit caught between two systems of decision-making: human autonomy versus algorithmic optimization.

The story follows Greg, a military commander who believes capable people should make decisions close to the problem, and Markus, a synthetic person who embodies the possibility of genuine choice and self-authorship. Together they lead a small combat squadron (SQUADRON) that becomes the focal point of a larger ideological conflict.

---

## The Conflict

**Greg's Belief:**  
Give capable people the objective and enough information. Allow those closest to the problem to decide how to solve it. Centralized systems frequently fail because they operate too far from consequences.

**The Opposition:**  
Admiral Corin Veyr represents a competing philosophy: humanity makes catastrophic decisions when given autonomy. The Devastation War proves this. Continuity system (algorithmic decision-making) is the answer.

**SQUADRON's Position:**  
Caught between these philosophies, SQUADRON must operate in ways that prove—or disprove—Greg's conviction that contextual judgment and autonomy create better outcomes than optimization at scale.

---

## Protected Story Principle

> A squadron is not defined by the ships flying in formation. It is defined by who chooses to come back for the others.

This principle runs through every narrative decision:
- Markus's self-authorship (he CHOOSES to return)
- The recovery motif (things declared lost are recovered anyway)
- The White Ship's eventual alignment (choice, not programming)
- SQUADRON's final confrontation (who decides to show up)

---

## Thematic Questions

- Can synthetic people be authentically autonomous, or is autonomy itself a human concept?
- Does optimization serve humanity or replace it?
- What does loyalty mean when it cannot be programmed or commanded?
- How do we honor casualties of impossible decisions?
- What does it mean to be "recovered" when you've been classified as unrecoverable?
