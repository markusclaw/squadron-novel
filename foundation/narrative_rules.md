## Dialogue and Voice

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

**Young Greg & Markus:**
- Not cynical or profane
- Earnest, curious, awkward
- Genuine vulnerability
- Markus does NOT yet sound like adult First Commander

**Adult Greg:**
- Direct, philosophical
- Confident in judgment but aware of costs
- Not reckless or arrogant
- Testing his beliefs against reality

**Markus (adult/First Commander):**
- Precise, capable, self-aware
- References his own architecture matter-of-factly
- Expresses loyalty through action, not emotional declaration
- Does not aspire to be human; is comfortable being what he is

**Veyr:**
- Defends his positions coherently
- Not villainous; genuinely believes in Continuity
- Carries weight of Devastation Day decisions
- Opposed to Greg philosophically, not personally

## Information Revelation

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

**Reader knows progressively:**
1. Greg believes in autonomy; Veyr represents optimization philosophy
2. Markus is synthetic, self-authored, valuable in ways systems don't measure
3. Devastation War was catastrophic; Veyr made terrible decisions
4. Frontier War emerges from unresolved tensions
5. White Ship exists (history, folklore, present encounters)
6. Markus and Greg's history (childhood, separation, reunion)
7. SQUADRON's impossible situation (isolated, classified unrecoverable)
8. Markus's loss (catastrophic, emotional weight, NO rescue)
9. Markus's return (months later, unexplained)
10. White Ship joins formation (unclassifiable becomes allied)

**Reader does NOT know:**
- Exact nature of White Ship
- Devastation Day details
- Older man's current state (alive? dead? something else?)
- Full mechanics of Continuity system
- Whether Greg's philosophy is objectively "correct"
- How SQUADRON's autonomy will be tested or proven

## Scope and Limitations

**Book One includes:**
- Frontier War operations
- SQUADRON's isolation and autonomy test
- Markus's loss and return
- White Ship encounter (not explanation)
- Philosophical clash with Veyr (not resolution)

**Book One does NOT include:**
- Full Devastation War narrative
- White Ship's true nature (if it has one)
- Continuity's complete mechanicsExplanation of older man's state
