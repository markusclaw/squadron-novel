# ADAM

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

---

## Overview

Greg's father. An industrial worker who recovered the damaged utility robot that would become Markus. Crucial to Markus's origin and early development.

---

## Background

**Work & Life:**
- Works at major industrial/logistics facility supporting the colony
- Facility supports colony infrastructure and operations
- Has mechanical knowledge and access to tools
- Casual, resourceful approach to problems

**Recovery of Markus:**
- Damaged utility robot written off at facility (economically unviable)
- Asks coworker what will happen to it
- Told: "You want it? Take it. Just wipe it before it leaves"
- Casually brings unit home (light enough to carry by built-in handle)
- Gives it to Greg as something to repair and work on
- Treats it like parent giving child a pet project

**Hardware Restoration:**
- Adam repairs hardware progressively over years
- Provides workshop space and tools
- Finds old M.A.R.K.U.S. software package in own collection
- Adapts M.A.R.K.U.S. to the recovered unit
- System activates; framework identifies itself as M.A.R.K.U.S.
- Greg hears "Markus" (name origin)

**Later Years:**
- Continues supporting Markus's development as Greg modifies systems
- Involved in hardware maintenance and improvements
- Enables Markus to physically grow through better bodies

---

## Relationship with Greg

**As Father:**
- Presents the opportunity (recovered robot)
- Trusts Greg to figure out how to use it
- Supports Greg's tinkering and modifications
- Allows Markus to become Greg's responsibility and companion

**Parenting Style:**
- Casual and resourceful
- Enables discovery rather than directing
- Comfortable with unconventional situations (raising boy with synthetic companion)

---

## Relationship with Markus

**Nature:**
- Hardware provider and maintainer
- Adult authority figure who enables Markus's growth
- Treats Markus as legitimate entity (not object)
- Participates in ongoing hardware modifications

**Significance:**
- First human to "recover" Markus (brought him home)
- Sets pattern for recovery motif
