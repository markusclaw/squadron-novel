# OLDER MAN

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

---

## Overview

The older young man from the House on the Hill. An important figure in Greg and Markus's childhood, influential mentor, later military commander who disappears during Devastation Day.

**Name:** ○ OPEN

---

## Early Life (Childhood Era)

**Background:**
- Several years older than Greg
- Lives in House on the Hill (large, influential family property)
- From wealthy, successful family
- Politically/socially connected to military/government/industrial circles
- Technically gifted, mechanically intuitive
- Accustomed to formal social settings

**Relationship with Young Greg & Markus:**
- Treats both naturally and without condescension
- Casually welcoming and gracious
- Encourages young Greg
- Treats Markus naturally (never as object of ridicule)
- Invites them into House Gathering without ceremony
- Greg likely overestimates closeness because of his age (intentional)
- Not explicitly framed as "best friend," but as mentor figure

**Hardware Contributions:**
- Helps improve Markus's physical hardware
- Improves legs, balance geometry, servos
- Demonstrates technical competence and care
- Contributes to Markus's physical growth

---

## Military Career

**Education & Advancement:**
- Graduates from elite military academy
- Obtains prestigious command appointment (likely around time of House Gathering)
- Eventually becomes significant squadron commander
- Rises through military ranks during Devastation War

**Devastating Day:**
- Disappears during or around Devastation Day (catastrophic triage event)
- May have been among those classified unrecoverable
- Formation under his command may have been sacrificed in Veyr's triage decision
- Appears only ambiguously in official memorial records

**Early Influence:**

- Absence of this mentor forces Greg to become independent
