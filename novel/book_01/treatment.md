# BOOK ONE TREATMENT

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

---

## High-Level Story

Greg commands SQUADRON during the Frontier War. When Relay connectivity fails, SQUADRON becomes isolated from central command and Continuity's oversight. Greg must prove his autonomy philosophy works. The story tests this through catastrophic loss (Markus), impossible decisions, and the appearance of an unclassifiable phenomenon (White Ship).

---

## Three-Act Structure

### ACT I: Establishment

**Setup:**
- Greg commands SQUADRON in Frontier War operations
- Relay connectivity strong; Continuity system maintains oversight
- Partnership with Markus is solid and functional
- Veyr represents opposing philosophy
- Kristina maintains strategic picture from carrier

**Inciting Incident:**
- Relay begins to fail (environmental? enemy action? ◆ PROVISIONAL)
- SQUADRON loses central connection
- Continuity cannot direct or optimize operations
- Greg must lead independently

**End of Act I:**
- Relay completely fails
- SQUADRON is truly isolated
- Kristina disappears from communications
- Test begins: does autonomy work without central direction?

---

### ACT II: Pressure

**Rising Action:**
- SQUADRON operates independently
- Greg's autonomy philosophy is tested
- Continuity calculates SQUADRON as suboptimal/unrecoverable
- Decisions are made without central review
- Outcomes challenge or validate autonomy approach

**Midpoint/Catastrophe:**
- Markus suffers catastrophic loss
- Classified as unrecoverable
- White Ship appears (substantial encounter)
- SQUADRON // 3 ACTIVE
- Greg faces impossible situation
- Months pass in absence of core anchor

**Low Point:**
- Markus is gone
- SQUADRON is fractured
- Veyr's optimizations begin to look correct
- Greg's philosophy is tested in its most brutal form

## Character Arcs (Overview)

**Greg:**
- Confident in autonomy philosophy → tested by loss → proven partially correct but incomplete
- Leadership through trust → loses Markus → must lead through grief → finds new form of leadership

**Markus:**
- Established as SQUADRON anchor → classified unrecoverable → returns → classifies White Ship as friendly
- Self-authored person demonstrates autonomy through loss and return

**Veyr:**
- Confident in Continuity → loses control of SQUADRON → confronted by autonomous success → position challenged but not destroyed
- Remains coherent in his philosophy despite outcomes

**Older Man:**
- Present through memory and absence
- Absence creates vacuum Greg must fill

**White Ship:**
- Unclassifiable presence → joins formation → becomes symbol of what systems cannot account for
