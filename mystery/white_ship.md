## Thematic Function

**The Unclassifiable:**
- Some things cannot be categorized
- Some things physically affect reality without being controllable or predictable
- Some things simply *are*
- Wisdom is sometimes accepting mystery rather than solving it

**For Markus:**
- Synthetic person classified systems once struggled with
- Eventually classifies unclassifiable presence as allied
- Acts as mirror: if Markus (once unclassifiable) accepts this presence, reader should consider what that means

**For Greg:**
- Tests autonomy philosophy beyond military context
- Cannot "solve" White Ship through contextual judgment
- Must accept something that resists optimization or explanation
- Forces growth beyond operational thinking
