# CONTINUITY

**Status:** ◆ PROVISIONAL

---

## Overview

Continuity is an algorithmic decision-making system developed after the Devastation War. It represents the opposing philosophy to Greg's autonomy-based approach. It is not "evil AI"—it is a coherent system defended by Admiral Corin Veyr.

---

## Origins

**When:**
- ● CANON — Developed AFTER Devastation War
- ● CANON — NOT the cause of Devastation War
- ◆ PROVISIONAL — Exact timeline of development TBD

**Why:**
- ● CANON — Response to catastrophic human command failures
- ● CANON — Senior commanders had to choose who could be saved
- ● CANON — These decisions (like Veyr's) created generational trauma
- ● CANON — Continuity developed partly to reduce dependence on human judgment in such situations
- ● CANON — Attempt to prevent civilization from depending upon one exhausted human making impossible calculations

**What Problem It Solves:**
- Reduces dependence on emotion, fatigue, bias, panic, favoritism, incomplete human judgment
- Calculates survival outcomes
- Removes personal guilt from individual decision-makers
- Provides optimization at scale

## What Continuity Is

**Nature:**
- ● CANON — System, not consciousness
- ● CANON** — Calculates and optimizes
- ● CANON** — Cannot be Markus's "sibling AI" or "evil opposite"
- ● CANON — Not secretly sentient
- ● CANON — Not possessed of its own agenda or desires
- ● CANON — Not equivalent to Markus

**Capabilities:**
- ◆ PROVISIONAL — Specific technical capabilities TBD
- ● CANON — Can classify situations and personnel
- ● CANON — Can calculate survival probabilities
- ● CANON — Can optimize resource allocation
- ● CANON — Can interface with military units through Relay
- ● CANON — Cannot understand or classify the unclassifiable (White Ship)
- ● CANON — Cannot predict or account for autonomous choice (Markus returning)

**Limitations:**
- ● CANON — Operates too far from ground-level consequences
- ● CANON — Cannot see contextual factors that matter to operators
- ● CANON — Cannot predict behavior based on loyalty or choice (non-optimized factors)
- ● CANON — Cannot classify anomalies it has no framework for
- ◆ PROVISIONAL — Other specific limitations to be developed

## Relay System

**Function:**
- ● CANON — Communication/coordination system connecting units to central command
- ● CANON — Carries Continuity's oversight and optimization
- ● CANON — Allows real-time tactical direction and resource allocation
- ● CANON — When Relay fails, Continuity loses contact and control

**Failure Event:**
- ● CANON — Relay fails during Frontier War

## Veyr's Defense

**Why Veyr Believes in Continuity:**
- ● CANON — Devastation War decision haunts him
- ● CANON — Continuity represents way to prevent such decisions from requiring one human to carry such weight
- ● CANON — Veyr is not wrong that Devastation was catastrophic
- ● CANON — Veyr is not wrong that better systems might prevent recurrence
- ● CANON — Veyr IS wrong about the cost (removes autonomy and contextual judgment)

**Veyr's Position:**
- ● CANON — Defends Continuity coherently, not as villain
- ● CANON — Genuinely believes in system
- ● CANON — Willing to bear costs of position
- ● CANON — Opposed to Greg philosophically, not personally malicious

## Greg's Opposition

**Why Greg Opposes Continuity:**
- ● CANON — Centralized systems operate too far from consequences
- ● CANON — Operators closest to problem have contextual knowledge systems lack
- ● CANON — Optimization removes judgment
- ● CANON — Autonomy + context = better outcomes (his belief)

**Greg's Test:**
- ● CANON — SQUADRON's isolation from Relay is the TEST
